# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Ghana managing everyday money across cash, bank, and mobile-money accounts.

## Product Purpose

Largent is a personal-finance app that helps people understand what they can spend each day by turning their budgets into a simple daily limit.

## Positioning

Largent makes a daily spending decision easier by translating budgets into a clear daily limit.

## Operating Context

People manage everyday money held in cash, bank, and mobile-money accounts, and use GHS balances to understand their finances.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- GHS-first account balances represent money held across cash, bank, and mobile-money accounts.
- The frontend is a mobile-first web PWA.
- Frontend work follows `AGENT.md` for API access, money handling, accessibility, and online-first writes.
- `DESIGN.md` remains the visual authority for the frontend.
- This frontend scope does not include changes to the API backend.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Largent. Preserve the incumbent visual identity and assets documented in `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

The frontend includes reference screenshots in `docs/design/` and the visual specification in `DESIGN.md`. No customer testimonials, benchmarks, or external proof claims were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make daily spending decisions understandable.
- Represent account balances accurately in GHS.
- Support the real places people keep money.
- Keep the mobile web experience usable when connectivity is limited.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, labeled inputs, visible focus, readable contrast, and touch targets that meet the frontend's 44px minimum.
