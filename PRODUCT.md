# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People using Largent as a personal finance management app to track income and expenses, plan spending, and build savings. Specific user segments and situations are not yet defined.

## Product Purpose

Largent helps people make better spending decisions before their money runs out. It supports tracking income and expenses, managing daily spending, planning budgets, monitoring financial habits, and building savings.

## Positioning

Largent goes beyond recording transactions: its daily spending system helps users identify high-spending days, while planning and progress tools support decisions before money runs out.

## Operating Context

Users can organize essential purchases, distribute a weekly food budget across weekdays, set savings goals, monitor progress, and review spending patterns through financial reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed capabilities: daily spending and high-spending-day insights; weekly food-budget planning across weekdays; priority-needs organization and progress tracking; savings goals and progress; financial reports.
- The existing repository documentation describes a mobile-first PWA for Ghana using Ghanaian cedi (GHS). Confirm that market and currency assumption before treating it as a durable product constraint.
- Specific supported account types, offline guarantees, install behavior, and additional markets have not been confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Largent. No additional voice or identity constraints have been established.

## Evidence on Hand

- The application includes a daily spending dashboard and features for budgets, prioritized needs, savings, and reports.
- The public-facing sample dashboard labels its figures as illustrative, not live account data.
- No confirmed testimonials, customer logos, benchmarks, or other marketing proof have been provided.

## Product Principles

- Help users decide what to do with their money before it runs out.
- Make daily spending patterns understandable.
- Support practical planning for essentials and savings goals.
- Show progress and spending patterns so users can adjust their habits.
