# Brag Plan: Largent

## What is this app?
Largent is a mobile-first personal finance app for Ghana that turns everyday spending into a clear daily plan so people can decide what they can spend before money runs short.

## The angle
This is a quiet but sharp product film about making the boring parts of money feel legible. The joke is that money management is usually abstract and stressful, while Largent turns it into a visible decision: daily limits, food budgets, essential purchases, and savings goals all sit in one place without the usual confusion.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Start with the product's strongest headline, full-size and unapologetic: "Make better money decisions before you spend." It lands immediately because it sounds like a real problem-solving promise, not generic fintech jargon.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The dashboard hero card surfaces the real user story: a total balance across cash, bank, and mobile money, with a daily spend number that feels concrete.
- The food budget scene turns a weekly budget into a weekday-by-weekday plan, making a vague spending goal feel practical.
- The goal/progress scene shows the app's second advantage: people can track essentials and savings while watching the money story unfold.

## Outro / punchline
Finish with the product name and the system-level promise: Largent helps people understand their money before it runs out. Short, specific, and premium.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result
- Open the app and see the money dashboard
- Spot the spend today / food budget / goal progress panels
- Understand the plan and adjust before money runs short

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: The pacing stays confident and clean, with strong visual hierarchy and restrained motion. The edit feels thoughtful, not hype-heavy, and the copy remains grounded in the product's real value proposition.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — opening headline — 4s
The first frame is a deep navy hero panel with a calm blue-to-indigo gradient and the line “Make better money decisions before you spend.” The copy is large and crisp. To the right, a balance card shows “Total balance” and “₵4,280.00” with smaller supporting text. The interface feels real and product-native.
Sequential/interaction: yes — dashboard card and supporting metric appear in sequence with a soft lift and fade
Audio intent: open with a confident but understated supportive pulse
Audio-coupled idea: card reveal + text settle
Music: warm bed with light pulse
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — food budget planning — 4s
The frame shifts to a detailed food budget panel: “Food budget,” a streak of spending against a weekly plan, and a progress bar with “₵185 of ₵300.” This makes the product feel directly useful before the money runs out.
Sequential/interaction: yes — number cards and progress fill animate in
Audio intent: building clarity, still calm and intentional
Audio-coupled idea: progress bar fill / card sequence
Music: continues with steady rhythm
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — high-spending days and priorities — 5s
A stacked card set introduces “Know which days cost the most” and “Track essential purchases and savings goals.” The app frame shows multiple small, readable labels and goal cards, reinforcing that the system turns clutter into decisions.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three cards or tiles land one by one
Audio intent: momentum, still precise
Audio-coupled idea: tile-by-tile reveal
Music: slightly fuller; subtle beat accent
Transition mood: subtle → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — results and payoff — 4s
A final hero composition centers the Largent brand and a concise value statement: the app keeps spending patterns, goals, and progress in view so decisions are easier. The final frame holds on the brand lockup with a small CTA line from the product.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: final calm lift into a clean end
Audio-coupled idea: final logo / title settle
Music: fade under final beat and drift out
Transition mood: polished → end
